Presentation Rubrics

Projects are scored out of 10 in each category. Use this as a guide when preparing your presentation!

Judging criteria

Presentation rubric by category and score band
Band 1–4 5–7 8–10
Technical execution Implementation is largely incomplete. Project struggles to meet its basic objectives, and the team demonstrates limited understanding of the code. Implements basic features with occasional bugs. Shows understanding of relevant coding concepts. Attempts to handle edge cases. Implements main features reliably with minimal bugs. Strong understanding of relevant programming concepts. Code takes edge cases into consideration and handles them appropriately.
Creativity Reproduces a basic example without adaptation, with little evidence of independent ideation. Customisation or modest extension of a known concept that has some identifiable creative elements. Unique ideas and/or novel features that meaningfully enhance the project. Identifiable creative elements.
Utility Purpose and use case of the project is unclear. Implementation decisions cannot be explained. Demonstrates a plausible use case and provides a reasonable proof of concept. Addresses a well-defined objective effectively. The team can explain their implementation decisions.
Presentation Speech is halting and/or difficult to understand most of the time. Speech is clear and intelligible most of the time. Speech is fluent and clear, with an appropriate pace throughout.
